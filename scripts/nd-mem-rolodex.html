What this is

Every memory you have stored, arranged as a drum of cards. You move through it in three levels — projects → districts → memories — and diving past the last one wraps you back to the first.

The location band at the top is where you are. 0^2 is your depth: how many times you have dived from the start. Each segment after it is a step you can click to jump straight back to.

The branch map on the left records every dive this session, including the ones you backed out of. Any node jumps you there, so an abandoned path is never lost.
